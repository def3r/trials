=== Design note ===

An earlier, exhaustive version of this test (deleted -- superseded, not
kept) tried every composite from n=4..127 at 3 trials each. It got killed
after 11+ minutes still running. Root cause: qubit count is a step
function of n's bit-length (9/13/17/21/25 qubits per doubling range), so
roughly half the composites in [4,127] (everything in [64,127]) land in
the most expensive 25-qubit tier -- and that sweep queued ~150 such calls,
each taking tens of seconds, before reaching any of the cheap sections.
The fix was sampling a few representative points per qubit tier instead of
exhaustive coverage, plus explicit output flushing so progress is visible
rather than buffered until exit.
